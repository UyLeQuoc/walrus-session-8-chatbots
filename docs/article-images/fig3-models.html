Cheap models recall fine. They stop being changed by what they recall.

One eval, two runs per model. It teaches five things in one session, throws the conversation away, then asks four questions in a fresh one. The fifth thing is not a fact — it is a stored instruction, answers in Vietnamese, and nothing in the new session mentions it.

The finding
The cheap models are a third of the price and no worse at retrieval. They are worse at letting what they retrieved change how they answer.
What to take from it
If your eval only asks did it quote the right fact, every model passes and you learn nothing. Include one item that must change how the answer is written.
Two runs per model is a small sample and is reported as one. Cost is the OpenRouter balance before and after each run, so it includes retries — not a figure derived from a price table.
